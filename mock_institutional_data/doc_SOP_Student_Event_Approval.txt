Student Activities Council: Standard Operating Procedure for Student Event and Venue Approval
SOP Reference: SAC/DJSCE/2026/SOP-02

1. Purpose:
To set out how student chapters and clubs obtain approval for events, workshops and competitions held on campus.

2. Trigger:
A student chapter or club plans an event that uses an institute venue or institute funds.

3. Preconditions:
- The chapter has a faculty advisor on record for the current academic year.
- The event date does not fall within the examination period published in the academic calendar.

4. Procedure:
1. The chapter submits the Event Proposal Form at least 15 days before the event, signed by the faculty advisor.
2. The Student Activities Council checks the date against the academic calendar and venue bookings.
3. Venue booking is confirmed by the Administrative Office once the Council approves.
4. Events with external speakers or more than 200 participants also need the Principal's approval.
5. Within 7 days after the event, the chapter submits an event report with attendance and expenses.

5. Guardrails:
- No event may be scheduled during the semester-end examination period.
- Collection of fees from participants requires a receipt issued through the institute account, never cash to students.
- The faculty advisor or a nominated faculty member must be present throughout the event.

6. Escalation:
Rejected proposals may be appealed to the Dean of Student Affairs.
