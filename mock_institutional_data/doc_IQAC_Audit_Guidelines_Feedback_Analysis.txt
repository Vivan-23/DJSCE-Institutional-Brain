Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC): Continuous Assessment and Course File Quality Guidelines
Notice Reference: IQAC/DJSCE/2026/CIR-14

1. Purpose:
To streamline teaching-learning documentation, outcome attainment evaluation, and student feedback mechanisms across all undergraduate engineering programs under autonomous status.

2. Student Feedback Review Norms:
- Student feedback is collected twice per semester: Mid-term feedback (Week 6) and End-term feedback (Week 13).
- Any course scoring below 75% on clarity of evaluation rubrics triggers an automatic departmental review.
- The IQAC committee requires faculty action-taken reports to be documented and archived in the departmental quality file within 10 days of feedback publication.

3. Continuous Evaluation Rubrics:
- Laboratory experiments must be graded on three criteria: Conceptual Preparedness (25%), Execution & Results (50%), and Viva-Voce & Analysis (25%).
- Marks must be recorded in the departmental continuous assessment portal within 7 working days of experiment completion.

4. Course File Standardization:
Every academic course file must contain:
1. Approved Syllabus with Course Outcomes (COs) and Bloom's Taxonomy levels.
2. CO to Program Outcome (PO) mapping matrix with justified correlation weights (1, 2, or 3).
3. Lecture conduction logbook and delivery plan.
4. Internal assessment question papers along with model answer keys and evaluation schemes.
5. Attainment calculation sheets detailing Direct and Indirect CO attainments.
6. Sample evaluated student answer scripts representing high, medium, and low performance bands.
