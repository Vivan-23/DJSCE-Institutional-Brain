# [DEMONSTRATION PROTOTYPE · SYNTHETIC INSTITUTIONAL SOP]
# Standard Operating Procedure: End-Semester Laboratory Practical Examination & External Moderation
# Target Organization: Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering (Demo Prototype)
# Document Identifier: DJSCE/EXAM/SOP-2026-03
# Notice: This is a synthetic procedure draft for evaluation and does not represent official college exam bylaws.

## 1. Trigger
End-of-semester autonomous practical examinations and viva-voce evaluations for undergraduate laboratory courses.

## 2. Preconditions
- Students must meet minimum laboratory attendance eligibility (75% minimum aggregate attendance or authorized medical condonation).
- Laboratory journal and certified experiment write-ups must be signed by the respective laboratory in-charge at least 5 days prior to the examination date.
- External and internal examiners must be appointed and approved by the Controller of Examinations and Academic Coordinator.

## 3. Examination Procedure Steps
1. The Examination Committee prepares sealed question paper sets containing balanced problem statements across basic, intermediate, and advanced experiment difficulty tiers.
2. The internal examiner verifies candidate roll numbers and laboratory identity badges upon student admission to the test lab.
3. Students draw lots or randomized problem statements generated from the examination portal.
4. Candidates complete coding, circuit wiring, or simulation tasks within the designated 2-hour duration without internet access, unless approved for cloud sandbox testing.
5. The external examiner and internal examiner jointly conduct oral viva-voce questioning focused on experiment methodology, theoretical foundations, and output validation.
6. Marks are compiled independently by both examiners on encrypted grade evaluation sheets according to the approved IQAC rubric.
7. Any discrepancy in scoring exceeding 15% between internal and external examiners requires immediate reconciliation before the sheet is signed.

## 4. Decision Rules & Thresholds
- Rule 1: If a student's laboratory journal is incomplete or uncertified, student is deferred until external examiner approval or supplementary session.
- Rule 2: If the variance between internal examiner and external examiner marks is <= 10%, average the score and record finalized grade.
- Rule 3: If the variance between internal and external marks is > 15%, both examiners must review student code output together and agree on a consensus mark within 24 hours.

## 5. Guardrails & Integrity Checks
- Examiner mark entry sheets must be sealed in tamper-evident envelopes immediately following the examination session.
- No electronic grading marks may be uploaded over unencrypted or public networks.
- Retesting or re-examination is strictly prohibited without written authorization from the Academic Coordinator and Principal.

## 6. Escalation Protocol
- For examiner non-appearance or hardware failures affecting >25% of candidates: Escalate immediately to the Examination In-charge (Prof. Karan Shah) and Department Lead (Dr. Rohan Mehta).
- For unresolvable grading disputes: Escalate to the Academic Coordinator (Dr. Aditi Rao).
