Examination Cell: Standard Operating Procedure for Revaluation and Photocopy Requests
SOP Reference: EXAM/DJSCE/2026/SOP-03

1. Purpose:
To define how the Examination Cell receives, verifies and settles student requests for a photocopy of an answer book or for revaluation of a semester-end examination script.

2. Trigger:
A student submits a revaluation or photocopy request after semester-end results are declared.

3. Preconditions:
- Results for the examination session have been formally declared.
- The request is filed within 7 calendar days of result declaration.
- The prescribed fee has been paid through the institute fee portal.

4. Procedure:
1. The Examination Cell clerk logs the request in the revaluation register and issues an acknowledgement number.
2. The clerk verifies fee payment and the 7-day window; late or unpaid requests are returned with the reason noted.
3. For photocopy requests, a scanned copy of the answer book is issued within 5 working days.
4. For revaluation, the script is masked (seat number hidden) and sent to an examiner other than the original evaluator.
5. If the revised marks differ from the original by 10% of the maximum marks or more, the script goes to a third examiner and the average of the two closest marks is taken.
6. The Controller of Examinations approves the final marks and the result is updated within 21 working days of the request.

5. Guardrails:
- The original examiner must never revaluate their own script.
- Marks can only increase or stay the same if the change is under 10% of maximum marks; any decrease requires the Controller's written approval.
- No revaluation is permitted for practical, oral or term-work components.

6. Escalation:
Disputes after revaluation are escalated to the Examination Grievance Committee, chaired by the Controller of Examinations.
