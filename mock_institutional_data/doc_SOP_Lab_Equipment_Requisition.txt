Department Laboratory Office: Standard Operating Procedure for Equipment Requisition and Breakage
SOP Reference: LAB/DJSCE/2026/SOP-07

1. Purpose:
To standardise how faculty request new laboratory equipment or consumables, and how breakage or loss by students is reported and recovered.

2. Trigger:
A faculty member needs equipment or consumables for a laboratory course, or a lab assistant records a breakage.

3. Procedure for requisition:
1. The faculty member fills the Lab Requisition Form with item, quantity, estimated cost and the course it supports.
2. The Lab-in-Charge checks existing stock in the departmental stock register before forwarding.
3. Requests under Rs. 25,000 are approved by the Head of Department.
4. Requests of Rs. 25,000 or more require three vendor quotations and approval of the Purchase Committee.
5. On delivery, the lab assistant enters the item in the dead-stock or consumables register within 3 working days.

4. Procedure for breakage:
1. The lab assistant records the breakage in the breakage register with the student's roll number and the date.
2. The student is informed in writing and recovery is charged at replacement cost.
3. Recovery amounts are adjusted against the student's caution deposit at the end of the academic year.

5. Guardrails:
- Equipment must not be moved between laboratories without an entry in both stock registers.
- Accidental breakage during a supervised experiment, certified by the faculty in charge, is not charged to the student.

6. Escalation:
Unresolved stock discrepancies are escalated to the Head of Department and reported at the annual stock verification.
