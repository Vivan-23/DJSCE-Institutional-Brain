Department of Computer Engineering — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When was the Computer Engineering department established?
A: The department was established in 2001.

Q: What was the initial intake of the department?
A: The initial intake was 30 students.

Q: How has the intake changed over the years?
A: In 2002-03, the intake was increased to 60, in 2011-12 to 120, and in 2021-22 to 180.

Q: When did the ME program start?
A: The ME program started in 2012-13 with an intake of 18 students.

Q: When did the Ph.D. program begin?
A: The Ph.D. program began in 2015-16 with an intake of 10 students.

Q: Who is the current Head of Department?
A: Dr. (Mrs.) Meera Narvekar is the current Head of Department.

Q: What is the vision of the Computer Engineering department?
A: To impart high quality education, promote research culture and inculcate ethical values to develop professionally competent engineers capable of meeting global challenges.

Q: What are the mission objectives?
A: Encourage research aptitude, develop soft skills and ethics, create awareness about sustainability, nurture technocrats for industry, and provide state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Q: What is the intake capacity for B.Tech?
A: The intake capacity is 180 students.

Q: What is the duration of the B.Tech program?
A: The program duration is 4 years.

Q: Which year was the program accredited by NBA?
A: The program was accredited in the Academic Year 2012-13.

Q: What courses are in Semester III?
A: Engineering Mathematics-III, Data Structures, Discrete Structures, Database Management Systems, Digital Electronics, Java Programming Lab, IPD-I (Audit), Constitution of India (Audit).

Q: What courses are in Semester IV?
A: Engineering Mathematics-IV, Formal Language & Automata Theory, Operating System, Analysis of Algorithms, Computer Networks, Universal Human Values, IPD-II (Audit).

Q: What courses are in Semester V?
A: Data Mining and Warehouse, Processor Organization and Architecture, Artificial Intelligence, Communication Lab, Department Electives, Python Lab, IPD-III (Credit).

Q: What courses are in Semester VI?
A: Software Engineering, Advanced Algorithm, Information Security, Electives like IoT, ML, Compiler Design, etc., IPD-IV (Credit), Environmental Studies (Audit).

Q: What courses are in Semester VII?
A: Digital Signal Processing, Distributed Computing, Deep Learning (Elective), Blockchain, Predictive Modeling, Institute Level Optional Course I, Project Stage I.

Q: What courses are in Semester VIII?
A: Web Intelligence, High Performance Computing, NLP, Software Architecture, Software Testing, Institute Level Optional Course II, Project Stage II.

Q: What programming languages are taught?
A: Java and Python are the main programming languages taught through lab courses.

Q: What facilities are provided in labs?
A: Labs are equipped with modern computer systems, licensed and open-source software, internet, OHPs, LCD projectors, scanners, and printers.

Q: How many faculty members are in the department?
A: There are 24 faculty members in the department.

Q: What are some department elective options in Semester V?
A: Advanced Operating System, Advanced DBMS, Network Engineering.

Q: Name 3 electives in Semester VI.
A: Internet of Things, Machine Learning, Compiler Design.

Q: What are some career options after this degree?
A: Careers in AI, Data Science, Software Development, Embedded Systems, and placements in MNCs.

Q: Can students pursue higher education abroad?
A: Yes, in institutions like IITs, IISc, and universities in the UK, US, Canada, and more.
