General Academic Policies — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the attendance policy?
A: Students are required to attend at least 75% of class sessions. Individual instructors may set stricter policies, which are stated in their course syllabus.

Q: Can I take a make-up exam if I miss an exam?
A: Make-up exams are typically allowed only for documented illness, family emergencies, or religious observances. Contact your instructor as soon as possible to request one.

Q: What are the exam policies?
A: Exam policies are set by individual instructors and outlined in the course syllabus. Final exams must be held during the scheduled examination period.

Q: When is the final exam schedule published?
A: Final exam schedules are published approximately one month before the examination period, on the examination notice board and the student portal.

Q: What does the academic integrity policy cover?
A: The academic integrity policy prohibits cheating, plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, and falsification of data. Violations can lead to failing grades, probation, or expulsion.

Q: How does grading work?
A: Courses use a standard grading scale. Some courses may be taken on a pass/fail basis with instructor approval; the deadline to change a grading option is the same as the add/drop deadline.

Q: What is the add/drop deadline for courses?
A: The add/drop deadline is typically two weeks after classes begin. After that, adding a course needs special permission and dropping is recorded on the transcript.

Q: How do I contact a faculty member or book advising?
A: Faculty office hours are listed in course syllabi and department pages. Advising appointments can be requested through the student portal or by emailing your assigned advisor.
