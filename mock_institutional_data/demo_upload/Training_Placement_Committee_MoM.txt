Training and Placement Committee Meeting: Campus Recruitment Drive Planning 2026-27
Date: October 15, 2026
Attendees: Dr. Aditi Rao, Prof. Karan Shah, Prof. Pooja Verma, Dr. Rohan Mehta

Dr. Aditi Rao: Good afternoon. The agenda today is planning the campus recruitment drive for the final-year Computer Engineering and Information Technology batches, and the pre-placement training schedule.
Prof. Karan Shah: Twelve companies have confirmed so far. Four more are expected to confirm by the end of this month, including two product companies that want students with cloud and data skills.
Dr. Rohan Mehta: Students have asked for mock interviews again this year. Last year's feedback said the aptitude sessions started too late, only two weeks before the first drive.
Dr. Aditi Rao: Decision: Pre-placement aptitude and coding training will start on November 2 and run for four weeks, three sessions per week.
Prof. Pooja Verma: Lab 4 can host the online coding assessments. The new Gigabit switch is installed, so we can run sixty students at a time.
Dr. Aditi Rao: Decision: All online coding assessments for the drive will be conducted in Lab 4 with a faculty invigilator present.
Prof. Karan Shah: We also need a clear eligibility rule. Several companies have asked for a minimum CGPA of 6.5 with no active backlogs.
Dr. Aditi Rao: Decision: The default eligibility criterion is a CGPA of 6.5 or above with no active backlogs, unless a company specifies otherwise.
Dr. Rohan Mehta: I will prepare the mock interview panel list with at least eight faculty members by October 22.
Prof. Karan Shah: I will send the consolidated company list and drive calendar to the Help Desk and the department offices by October 20.
Prof. Pooja Verma: I will run a load test of the assessment platform in Lab 4 with sixty machines by October 24.
Dr. Aditi Rao: I will publish the eligibility rules and training schedule as a notice to final-year students by October 18.
Dr. Aditi Rao: Thank you everyone. We will review progress in the next meeting on October 29.
