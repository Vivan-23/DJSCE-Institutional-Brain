Course Proposal: Applied Generative AI and Machine Learning (Elective IV)
Program: Bachelor of Technology in Computer Engineering
Autonomous Regulation: DJSCE-AR-2026
Department: Computer Engineering

1. Course Objectives:
To provide students with rigorous foundations in deep neural representations, transformer architectures, diffusion models, and retrieval-augmented generation systems.

2. Prerequisite Courses:
- CS301: Advanced Data Structures & Algorithms (Grade C or above)
- CS304: Applied Linear Algebra & Probability Theory
- CS402: Fundamentals of Machine Learning

3. Syllabus Modules:
- Module 1: Mathematical Foundations of Attention Mechanisms (6 hours)
- Module 2: Encoder-Decoder & Decoder-Only Transformer Architectures (8 hours)
- Module 3: Representation Learning, Embeddings, and Vector Databases (ChromaDB, FAISS) (8 hours)
- Module 4: Fine-Tuning Paradigms: LoRA, QLoRA, and Instruction Tuning (8 hours)
- Module 5: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Architectures and Factuality Evaluation (6 hours)
- Module 6: Safety Guardrails, Hallucination Mitigation, and Ethical AI Governance (6 hours)

4. Laboratory Infrastructure:
- Lab Venue: Advanced Computing Laboratory (Lab 302)
- Workstation Specs: 32 GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 4080 (16GB VRAM), Ubuntu 24.04 LTS
- Approved Batch Capacity: 60 students in two lab batches of 30 students each

5. Assessment Scheme:
- Continuous Assessment Test (CAT 1 & CAT 2): 20 marks
- Mini-Project Evaluation using Rubric R3: 20 marks
- Laboratory Practical Examination & Oral Viva: 25 marks
- End Semester Written Examination: 60 marks (converted to 50 weightage)
