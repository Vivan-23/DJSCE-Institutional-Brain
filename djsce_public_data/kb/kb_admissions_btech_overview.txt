B.Tech Undergraduate Admissions, Intake, and Eligibility

Q: What is the sanctioned intake for B.Tech programmes at DJSCE?
A: The sanctioned intake across undergraduate engineering branches for First Year B.Tech is:
- Computer Engineering: 180 seats
- Information Technology: 180 seats
- Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering: 180 seats
- Mechanical Engineering: 180 seats
- Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science): 180 seats
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: 120 seats
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Data Science: 60 seats
- Computer Science and Engineering (IoT and Cyber Security with Block Chain Technology): 60 seats
Total B.Tech Sanctioned Intake: 1140 seats. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-btech)

Q: How are admissions conducted for First Year B.Tech?
A: First Year B.Tech admissions are conducted through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) rounds administered by the State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell), Government of Maharashtra, as well as Institutional Level Seats (ILS) and vacancy rounds conducted strictly as per DTE/CET Cell merit guidelines. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-btech; https://www.djsce.ac.in/admission-notices-2025)

Q: Are admissions offered for Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE)?
A: Yes, admissions to Direct Second Year B.Tech are offered to eligible diploma/B.Sc. candidates through the CAP process administered by the CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-2nd-year-direct)
