Department of CSE (IoT and Cyber Security with Block Chain Technology)

Q: When was the CSE (IoT and Cyber Security with Block Chain Technology) programme established?
A: The Department was established in the academic year 2021. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-science-engineering-iot-cyber-security-with-block-chain-technology)

Q: What is the sanctioned intake and degree awarded?
A: The programme has a sanctioned intake of 60 seats, awarding the B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (IoT and Cyber Security with Block Chain Technology) degree. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-science-engineering-iot-cyber-security-with-block-chain-technology)

Q: What specialized domains are covered in this programme?
A: The curriculum covers Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT) protocols and architecture, Cryptography, Network and Cyber Security, Ethical Hacking, Blockchain Consensus mechanisms, and Smart Contracts. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-science-engineering-iot-cyber-security-with-block-chain-technology)
