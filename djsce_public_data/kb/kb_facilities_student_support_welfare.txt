Student Welfare, Counselling, Grievance Cell, and Divyangjan Support

Q: Is psychological and personal counselling available on campus?
A: Yes, DJSCE provides professional psychological counselling services open to all students and staff through dedicated SVKM counsellors to assist with emotional wellbeing, academic stress, and personal guidance. Official contact details are published on the DJSCE website. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/counselling)

Q: How can students submit grievances?
A: Students can raise academic or administrative concerns through the official Student Grievance Redressal mechanism and online Grievance portal, which ensures objective and timely evaluation by the Grievance Cell. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/grievance-cell)

Q: What facilities are provided for Divyangjan (Persons with Disabilities)?
A: The institution provides accessible campus facilities including ramps, lifts, designated restrooms, and scribe assistance during examinations per regulatory norms. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/divyangjan)
