M.Tech and Ph.D. Programme Admissions and Research Centers

Q: Which M.Tech programmes are offered at DJSCE?
A: DJSCE offers 2-year Master of Technology (M.Tech) programmes in:
- M.Tech in Computer Engineering
- M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering
- M.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (Manufacturing Systems)
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-mtech; https://www.djsce.ac.in/pg-computer-engineering)

Q: Which Ph.D. research centers are recognized at DJSCE?
A: DJSCE is a recognized Research Centre of the University of Mumbai for doctoral research leading to Ph.D. (Technology) in:
- Ph.D. in Computer Engineering
- Ph.D. in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering
- Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-phd)

Q: What is the eligibility criteria for Ph.D. admission?
A: Candidates must possess a relevant Master's Degree (M.E./M.Tech) from a recognized University and have qualified the Ph.D. Entrance Test (PET) conducted by the University of Mumbai or hold a valid GATE/NET score or equivalent exemption as per University and UGC regulations. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-phd)
