Department of Mechanical Engineering

Q: When was the Department of Mechanical Engineering established?
A: The Department of Mechanical Engineering was established in 2011 with 60 seats, expanded to 120 seats, and further increased to 180 seats in 2022. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-mechanical-engineering)

Q: What is the degree awarded and accreditation status?
A: It offers a 4-year B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering and is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-mechanical-engineering)

Q: What are the key laboratories and subject domains?
A: Core subjects include Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials, CAD/CAM/CAE, Machine Design, Robotics, Mechatronics, and Manufacturing Processes, supported by modern workshops and simulation software. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-mechanical-engineering)
