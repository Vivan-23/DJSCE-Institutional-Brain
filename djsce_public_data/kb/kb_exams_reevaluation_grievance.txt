Re-evaluation, Photocopy Procedure, and Exam Grievance Redressal

Q: What is the procedure for seeking re-evaluation or a photocopy of an answer book?
A: A learner not satisfied with their Semester End Examination result may apply online through the Student SAP Portal for a Photocopy of the evaluated answer book and/or Re-evaluation within the specified timeframe following result declaration. Offline applications are not entertained. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: Does re-evaluation apply to continuous assessments or practical exams?
A: No. The grievance redressal mechanism applies exclusively to Semester End Examinations (theory papers) and does NOT apply to continuous assessment, term tests, practicals, viva voce, or projects. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: When is a change of marks accepted in re-evaluation?
A: Re-evaluation marks are accepted if the revised marks increase or decrease by 10% or more of the maximum marks assigned to that paper, in which case the original marks are rendered null and void and the re-evaluated marks stand. If the change allows the candidate the benefit of passing the course, the re-evaluated marks are accepted regardless of the 10% threshold; the same applies if the change results in a change of grade in that paper or a change in the overall result, with or without grace marks. If the difference between the original marks (without grace marks) and the revaluation marks exceeds 20% of the maximum marks, a second revaluation is done by a third examiner, and the average of the second and third examiners' marks is final. If a learner who originally obtained a pass grade gets a fail grade after the first re-evaluation, a second re-evaluation by a third examiner is conducted, and the average of the second and third examiners' marks is final. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: Can a student receive a photocopy of a re-evaluated answer book?
A: No. Photocopies of re-evaluated answer books are strictly not provided to learners. The outcome of the revaluation is final and binding. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)
