Honours and Minor Degree Programme Regulations

Q: Who is eligible to opt for an Honours or Minor Degree programme?
A: Regular engineering students who have no live or dead backlog ("F" grade) in Semester I and II, and who possess a CGPA of 7.50 and above based on Semesters I and II, are eligible. For Direct Second Year (DSE) admitted learners, an aggregate percentage of 70% and above is required. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: How many Honours or Minor programmes can a student pursue simultaneously?
A: An eligible student can opt for a maximum of one Honours or one Minor programme at any point in time during regular engineering studies. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: In what timeframe must the Honours/Minor degree be completed?
A: The learner must complete the Honours/Minor degree requirements within the stipulated four semesters as offered in the academic curriculum. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)
