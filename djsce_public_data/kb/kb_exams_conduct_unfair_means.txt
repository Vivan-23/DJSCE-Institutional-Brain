Examination Hall Discipline, Scribe Guidelines, and Unfair Means Rules

Q: What items are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall?
A: Learners are forbidden from carrying books, notes, scribbled papers, mobile phones, smart watches, digital storage devices, electronic gadgets, or unauthorized items into the examination hall. Confiscation and unfair means inquiry will follow any possession. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: What is the procedure when suspected unfair means is detected?
A: The junior supervisor confiscates the copy material and answer sheet, issues a fresh answer sheet for the remaining duration (upon an undertaking from the candidate), and marks the incident on the Unfair Means Form. The matter is referred to the Unfair Means Committee for hearing and formal disciplinary decision. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: Is scribe facility provided for persons with disabilities?
A: Yes, learners with permanent or temporary physical disability can avail of a scribe and compensatory time in accordance with College and University guidelines by submitting a formal medical application prior to the exam. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)
