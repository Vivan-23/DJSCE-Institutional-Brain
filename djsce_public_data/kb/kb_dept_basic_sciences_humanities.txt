Department of Basic Sciences and Humanities

Q: What is the role of the Department of Basic Sciences and Humanities?
A: The department conducts foundation courses for all First Year B.Tech engineering disciplines, including Applied Mathematics, Applied Physics, Applied Chemistry, Communication Skills, and Universal Human Values. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/science-humanities)

Q: How does the department support student orientation?
A: It organizes student induction programmes, diagnostic assessments, bridge courses, and foundational laboratory experiences to prepare incoming students for rigorous engineering curricula. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/science-humanities)
