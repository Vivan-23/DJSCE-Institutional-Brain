SVKM Trust and Institutional Governance Structure

Q: What is SVKM?
A: Shri Vile Parle Kelavani Mandal (SVKM) is a Public Charitable Trust registered under the Society's Registration Act and Bombay Public Trust Act, established in 1934. It manages numerous prestigious educational institutions including DJSCE. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/svkm-overview)

Q: What are the statutory governing bodies of DJSCE under autonomy?
A: As an Autonomous institution, DJSCE's governance includes Statutory Committees: Governing Body, Academic Council, Finance Committee, and Board of Studies (BOS) for each academic department. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/organization-governance)

Q: What is the role of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)?
A: The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at DJSCE ensures the internalization and institutionalization of quality enhancement initiatives, academic audits, curriculum improvements, and quality benchmarks as per NAAC and UGC guidelines. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/iqac)

Q: What non-statutory committees operate at DJSCE?
A: DJSCE maintains institutional committees including the Planning and Evaluation Committee, Grievance Redressal Committee, Anti-Ragging Committee, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), and Examination & Evaluation Committee. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/organization-governance)
