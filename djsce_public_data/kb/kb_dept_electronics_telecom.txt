Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (EXTC)

Q: When was the Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering established?
A: DJSCE started the EXTC department in 1999 with an intake of 30 seats, which grew to 60 seats, and currently has a sanctioned intake of 180 seats. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-electronics-telecommunication-engg)

Q: What degree is awarded and what is its accreditation status?
A: It offers a 4-year B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering and is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-electronics-telecommunication-engg)

Q: What are the core subject domains in EXTC?
A: The department offers training in Signals and Systems, RF and Microwave Engineering, Digital Communication, VLSI Design, Embedded Systems, Wireless Networks, and Signal Processing. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-electronics-telecommunication-engg)
