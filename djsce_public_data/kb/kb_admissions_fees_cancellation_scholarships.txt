Fee Structure, Cancellation Rules, and Scholarships Policy

Q: How is the fee structure for courses determined at DJSCE?
A: Fees for undergraduate and postgraduate courses at DJSCE are determined and approved by the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA), Government of Maharashtra. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-btech)

Q: What is the cancellation and refund policy for newly admitted students?
A: Admission cancellation rules and processing fees are governed strictly in accordance with the directives and guidelines issued by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE) / State CET Cell, Maharashtra, and University of Mumbai for the respective academic year. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-btech)

Q: What scholarships and freeships are available to DJSCE students?
A: Eligible students can apply for:
- Government of Maharashtra Social Welfare Department scholarships/freeships (via MahaDBT portal) for SC, ST, VJNT, OBC, and SBC categories.
- Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC) for economically weaker sections.
- Merit-cum-Means scholarships and private foundation trusts including Narotam Sekhsaria Foundation scholarship notices published on the college portal.
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/admissions-1st-year-btech)
