10-Point Grading Scheme, SGPA, and CGPA Calculation Norms

Q: What grading system is used at DJSCE?
A: A 10-point letter grading system is followed for all Under Graduate and Post Graduate programmes under autonomy. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: What are the letter grades and corresponding grade points (batches admitted in AY 2024-25 onwards)?
A: The grading scale is:
- Marks 90 to 100%: Grade Point 10 | Letter Grade: O (Outstanding)
- Marks 80 to 89.99%: Grade Point 9 | Letter Grade: A+ (Excellent)
- Marks 70 to 79.99%: Grade Point 8 | Letter Grade: A (Very Good)
- Marks 60 to 69.99%: Grade Point 7 | Letter Grade: B+ (Good)
- Marks 55 to 59.99%: Grade Point 6 | Letter Grade: B (Above Average)
- Marks 50 to 54.99%: Grade Point 5 | Letter Grade: C (Average)
- Marks 40 to 49.99%: Grade Point 4 | Letter Grade: P (Pass)
- Marks Less than 40%: Grade Point 0 | Letter Grade: F (Fail)
- Absent: Grade Point -- | Letter Grade: AB (Fail)
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: How is the SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) calculated?
A: SGPA = (Sum of Product of Grade Point and Credit for each course) / (Sum of Credits for all courses in that semester). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: How is the CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) calculated?
A: CGPA = (Sum of Product of Grade Point and Credit for each course across all semesters) / (Sum of Credits assigned across all semesters taken together). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: Is there a different grading table for batches admitted before AY 2024-25?
A: Yes. The Exam Manual has a separate grading matrix for batches admitted prior to AY 2024-2025, with different lower marks bands (including 45-49.99 and 40-44.99). Refer to the Exam Manual for the full table. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)
