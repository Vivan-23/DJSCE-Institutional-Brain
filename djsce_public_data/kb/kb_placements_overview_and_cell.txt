Training & Placement Cell Structure, Roles, and Activities

Q: What is the role of the Training and Placement Cell at DJSCE?
A: The Training and Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment, pre-placement training, resume verification, interview coordination, soft skill development, and industry-institute interactions for students across all disciplines. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/placements-hs)

Q: How is soft skill and pre-placement training conducted?
A: DJSCE conducts structured Value Added Programs (VAP), pre-placement aptitude training, coding workshops, technical resume building, mock interviews, and corporate etiquette training led by SVKM trainers and industry experts. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/soft-skill-training)

Q: What is the internship policy for students?
A: DJSCE encourages students to pursue internships during academic breaks in accordance with the AICTE and institutional internship guidelines, providing academic credits and industry exposure. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/placements-hs)

Q: Where are official placement cell contact details available?
A: Official contact details are published on the DJSCE website. The office of the Training & Placement Officer coordinates placement inquiries directly. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/placements-hs)
