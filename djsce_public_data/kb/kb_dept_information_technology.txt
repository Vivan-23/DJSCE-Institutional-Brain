Department of Information Technology

Q: When was the Department of Information Technology established?
A: The Department of Information Technology was established in 1999. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-information-technology)

Q: What is the duration, intake, and degree offered?
A: The programme is a 4-year undergraduate course with a sanctioned intake of 180 seats, awarding the B.Tech in Information Technology degree. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-information-technology)

Q: What is the accreditation status of the IT department?
A: The B.Tech Information Technology programme is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-information-technology)

Q: What are the key focus areas in the IT curriculum?
A: The IT curriculum focuses on Data Structures and Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming, Database Systems, Computer Networks, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, and Web and Mobile Technologies. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-information-technology)
