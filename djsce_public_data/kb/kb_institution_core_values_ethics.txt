Core Values, Research Policy, and Academic Ethics

Q: What are the Core Values of DJSCE?
A: The core values of DJSCE are:
- Holistic Approach: Promoting a value-based culture and embracing a holistic approach in daily functioning.
- Integrity: Instilling strong moral values such as honesty, uprightness, self-discipline, perseverance, and sincerity.
- Excellence: Striving for high standards in academic learning, technical competence, and research innovation.
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/core-values)

Q: What is the research policy at DJSCE?
A: The research policy aims to strengthen institutional capacity for strategic and operational research planning, create and administer research funds, encourage faculty and student publications, file intellectual property, and promote multidisciplinary research projects. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/research-policy)

Q: What code of ethics governs research at DJSCE?
A: DJSCE enforces strict adherence to academic integrity, avoidance of plagiarism, ethical conduct in experimental data generation, and institutional review through the Research Assessment and Ethics Committees. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/research-code-of-ethics)
