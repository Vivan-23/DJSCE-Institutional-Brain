Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering (DJSCE) Overview & Status

Q: What is DJSCE and when was it established?
A: Shri Vile Parle Kelavani Mandal's (SVKM) Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering (DJSCE) was established in the year 1994. It is affiliated to the University of Mumbai. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/djsce-overview)

Q: What is the autonomy status of DJSCE?
A: DJSCE is an Autonomous Institution, granted autonomy by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the University of Mumbai for a period of 10 years starting from Academic Year 2019-20 till 2028-29. Subsequently, the University of Mumbai further granted Empowered Autonomous status to the college for a period of 10 years till the Academic Year 2033-34. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/djsce-overview)

Q: What is the NAAC accreditation status of DJSCE?
A: The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) has accredited DJSCE with a CGPA of 3.18 out of 4, i.e., with an "A" Grade for a period of 5 years with effect from 18th October 2019. The page states that this validity was extended up to 31st December 2024 and subsequently by a further 3 months; it does not state a later end date. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/djsce-overview)

Q: What academic programmes are run by DJSCE?
A: For the A.Y. 2026-27, the college runs 8 undergraduate (B.Tech) programmes, 3 postgraduate (M.Tech) programmes, and 3 Ph.D. research programmes affiliated to the University of Mumbai. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/djsce-overview)

Q: Is DJSCE permanently affiliated to the University of Mumbai?
A: Yes, DJSCE is permanently affiliated to the University of Mumbai starting from the Academic Year 2017-18. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/highlights)
