Professional Student Chapters, Technical Clubs, and Cultural Teams

Q: Which professional student chapters operate at DJSCE?
A: Active technical chapters include:
- DJS ACM & ACM SIGAI (Association for Computing Machinery)
- DJS CSI (Computer Society of India)
- DJS IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
- DJS IETE (Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers)
- DJS ISHRAE (Indian Society of Heating Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Engineers)
- DJS ISME (Indian Society of Manufacturing Engineers)
- DJS SAE (Society of Automotive Engineers)
- DJS S4DS (Society for Data Science)
- DJS ISACA (Information Systems Audit and Control Association)
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/)

Q: Which technical and coding teams represent the college?
A: Special interest technical teams include:
- DJS Unicode (Coding & Web/Mobile Development)
- DJS Codestars (Competitive Programming)
- DJS DJinit.ai & DJS Synapse (AI & Machine Learning)
- DJS Antariksh (Martian Rover Team)
- DJS Racing, DJS Karting, DJS Kronos, and DJS Helios (Automotive teams)
- DJS Robocon (Robotics)
(Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/)

Q: Which cultural and literary societies are active?
A: Creative and social clubs include DJS Trinity (Annual Socio-Cultural-Sports Festival), DJS Beats (Music), DJS Dhadak (Dance), DJS Aura (Drama), DJS LIT (Literature), DJS Express (Debate), DJS MUNSOC (Model United Nations), and DJS N.S.S. (National Service Scheme). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/)
