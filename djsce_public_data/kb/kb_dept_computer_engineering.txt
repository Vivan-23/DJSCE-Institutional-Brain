Department of Computer Engineering

Q: When was the Department of Computer Engineering established?
A: The Department of Computer Engineering was established in 2001. Initially started with 30 seats, intake was increased to 60 in 2002-03, and subsequently increased to 180 seats. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-engineering)

Q: What is the duration, intake, and degree offered?
A: The programme is a 4-year undergraduate course with an intake of 180 seats, awarding the B.Tech in Computer Engineering degree. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-engineering)

Q: What is the accreditation status of the Computer Engineering department?
A: The B.Tech Computer Engineering programme is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-engineering)

Q: What core subjects are included in the autonomous curriculum?
A: The curriculum covers Engineering Mathematics, Data Structures, Discrete Structures, Database Management Systems, Operating Systems, Analysis of Algorithms, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Distributed Computing. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-engineering)

Q: Are departmental electives and honors/minors offered?
A: Yes, departmental electives include Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Big Data Infrastructure, Internet of Things, Natural Language Processing, Blockchain Technology, and Advanced Operating Systems, alongside honors specializations. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/ug-computer-engineering)
