Student Attendance Rules and Norms for Examination Eligibility

Q: What is the mandatory attendance requirement at DJSCE?
A: A learner is eligible for appearing at the Semester End Examination only if s/he fulfills the criteria of at least 75% attendance as an average of all the courses taken together in every semester, with a minimum of 70% attendance required in each individual course. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: How often is attendance displayed to learners?
A: The Attendance Committee displays the attendance for the previous month on the college notice boards and the learner portal during the first week of every month. Learners must report discrepancies within 3 days. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: What attendance credit is provided for extracurricular or co-curricular participation?
A: Learners officially representing the college in approved competitions, sports, or cultural activities with prior permission from the Principal receive attendance credit up to a maximum of 5% in the semester for the missed sessions. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)

Q: What is the power of condonation for attendance shortage?
A: The Principal has the authority to condone attendance deficiency up to an additional 5% per programme on genuine grounds. No condonation is permissible if attendance falls below 70% in any individual course; however, cases below 70% on grounds of genuine medical emergencies or other extreme exigencies may be referred by the Principal, with recommendations, to an Appellate Authority committee at the SVKM Management level. (Source: https://www.djsce.ac.in/docs/exams/Exam%20Manual%20with%20sign.pdf)
