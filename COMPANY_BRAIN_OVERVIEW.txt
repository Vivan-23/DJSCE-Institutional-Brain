1. PROJECT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project is organized as a production-grade full-stack mono-repo with a
FastAPI backend, a Next.js (React 19) frontend, multi-database storage engines
(PostgreSQL, ChromaDB, Neo4j, Redis), and background Celery workers.

2. TECHNOLOGIES, MODELS & ARCHITECTURE USED
================================================================================

A. CORE PROGRAMMING LANGUAGES & FRAMEWORKS
- Python 3.11: Powers the entire backend service, asynchronous pipelines, and AI engines.
- FastAPI (>=0.111.0): High-performance, async REST API server with OpenAPI docs.
- SQLModel / SQLAlchemy: Python ORM blending SQLAlchemy with Pydantic type safety.
- TypeScript / Next.js 16 / React 19: Modern server and client components.
- Tailwind CSS v4: Modern styling layer for the human review and assistant UI.

B. POLYGLOT STORAGE ARCHITECTURE (Multi-Database System)
1. PostgreSQL 16 (Relational Store):
   - Stores core operational records: Users, Employees, Meetings, Fragments,
     Commitments, Commitment Updates, CheckIns, Skills, Skill Citations, and Audit Logs.
   - Uses JSON-serialized text storage for arrays/dicts, allowing seamless SQLite fallback in tests.
2. ChromaDB (Vector Store):
   - Stores 1024-dimensional dense vector embeddings of every ingested Fragment.
   - Enables semantic similarity search, RAG retrieval, and fragment clustering.
3. Neo4j 5.18 (Graph Database):
   - Stores knowledge graph entities (Employees, Teams, Projects, Documents) and
     relationships (WORKS_ON, MANAGES, MENTIONED_IN) for structural company reasoning.
4. Redis 7 (Message Broker & Cache):
   - Task queue broker and state storage for Celery background jobs.

C. AI & MACHINE LEARNING MODELS USED IN CODE
1. Large Language Model (LLM):
   - Provider: Groq API client (`groq>=0.9.0`) with low-latency inference.
   - Default Model: `meta-llama/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct` (configurable to Llama-3.1/3.3/etc.).
   - Responsibilities:
     * Transcript Extraction: Parses speakers, generates executive summaries, extracts
       decisions, and identifies commitments with owners and deadlines.
     * Procedure / Skill Synthesis: Groups related fragments and synthesizes formal,
       executable skills (triggers, preconditions, step-by-step actions, decision rules,
       guardrails, and escalation protocols).
     * Company PA Check-In & Follow-ups: Conducts natural, empathetic end-of-day
       conversations, maps casual human speech to formal commitment updates, and writes
       new commitments without forcing employees into form-filling.
     * Contradiction / Freshness Engine: Evaluates recent company fragments against
       approved skills to detect obsolete rules, threshold changes, or procedural drift.
     * RAG Chat: Answers questions over company fragments with cited sources.
2. Embedding Model:
   - Model: `BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5`
   - Library: `sentence-transformers` (>=3.0.1) backed by PyTorch (`torch>=2.3.1`).
   - Function: Generates normalized 1024-dimensional semantic embeddings for all company
     fragments to power vector clustering and hybrid search.
3. Named Entity Recognition (NER) Model:
   - Model: `en_core_web_sm`
   - Library: `spacy` (>=3.7.4).
   - Function: Identifies real-world entities (PERSON, ORG, PRODUCT) from uploaded documents
     and meeting transcripts to populate the Neo4j graph nodes and edges.
4. Natural Language Rule-Based Processing:
   - Custom Date Parser (`app/services/date_parser.py`): Parses natural expressions like
     "by Friday", "next week", "in 3 days", or ISO dates into strict UTC timestamps.

D. ASYNCHRONOUS ORCHESTRATION & BACKGROUND JOBS
- Celery 5.4 + Celery Beat:
  * `process_transcript`: Background processing of uploaded audio/text transcripts.
  * `process_document`: Chunking and vectorizing PDF/DOCX/TXT files.
  * `run_daily_active_intelligence`: Daily scan for project bottlenecks and roadblocks.
  * `run_daily_checkins`: Evening trigger preparing end-of-day PA check-ins per timezone.
  * `run_commitment_followups`: Scans commitments inactive for >48 hours to generate nudges.
  * `run_skill_extraction`: Nightly clustering of newly ingested fragments into candidate skills.
  * `run_freshness_scan`: Periodic audit of approved skills against new company evidence.

E. SECURITY & ACCESS CONTROL
- Authentication: JWT tokens with HS256 encryption.
- Password Security: Passlib with bcrypt hashing.
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict tiered permissions: `employee` < `manager` < `admin`.

3. COMPREHENSIVE USE CASES OF COMPANY BRAIN
================================================================================

Based on the architecture and implementations in this codebase, here are the
core and extended real-world use cases:

1. Autonomous AI Agent Enablement (Executable SOP Generation):
   - Rather than giving an AI agent free reign or forcing humans to manually write prompts,
     Company Brain compiles messy real-world conversations into machine-executable `SKILL.md`
     files.
   - Agents (Claude Code, Antigravity, CrewAI, AutoGen, LangChain) fetch approved skills via
     `GET /api/v1/skills/{id}/file` and execute operational workflows with deterministic
     guardrails, decision trees, and escalation contacts.

2. Automated Meeting Action-Item Tracking & Accountability:
   - Ingests meeting transcripts (from Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Fireflies, Otter).
   - Automatically identifies who promised what, the exact deadline, and the context.
   - Saves human project managers from manually typing minutes and chasing assignees.

3. Zero-Form Personal Assistant (The "Company PA"):
   - Eliminates tedious status-reporting software and questionnaires.
   - At the end of the day (e.g., 5:00 PM), employees have a quick 2-minute chat with their
     in-app PA: "I finished the billing bug fix, but I'm waiting on legal for the contract."
   - The PA automatically marks commitments as 'done' or 'blocked' and captures new evidence.
   - Proactively nudges employees on stalled tasks in natural language without sounding robotic.

4. Procedural Drift & Freshness Detection (Automatic Anti-Rot for Company Knowledge):
   - Standard internal wikis (Notion, Confluence) go out of date within weeks because nobody
     updates them.
   - Company Brain continuously compares new meeting discussions and check-ins against approved
     skills. If a meeting decides "We no longer offer $50 refunds; the new limit is $20," the
     freshness engine immediately flags the refund skill as "stale", notifies the owner, and
     prevents autonomous agents from executing obsolete refund limits.

5. Risk & Bottleneck Early Warning System (Active Intelligence):
   - Analyzes progress reports, roadblocks, and overdue commitments across all teams.
   - Detects when multiple employees are blocked by the same team, API, or decision, raising
     automated "Active Insights" on the management feed before projects derail.

6. Instant Employee Onboarding & Institutional Knowledge Preservation:
   - When key engineers, support leads, or executives leave the company, their implicit knowledge
     usually disappears with them.
   - Company Brain preserves how decisions are made, who has authority, and the exact procedures
     used, cited directly back to the original discussions. New hires can review the skills or
     query the RAG chat to understand organizational practices immediately.

7. Enterprise Compliance, Evidence Citation & Human-in-the-Loop Governance:
   - Every single line in a generated skill is linked back to a `Fragment` citation (meeting ID,
     timestamp, speaker).
   - Managers must review, edit, and click "Approve" before an automated procedure can be deployed
     to autonomous agents.
   - Full append-only `AuditLog` records who extracted, edited, approved, or executed every skill.

4. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT'S USP (UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITION)?
================================================================================

Most enterprise AI projects are either:
  (a) Passive Q&A chatbots (Enterprise Search / RAG over docs), or
  (b) Autonomous agent frameworks that lack real company context and hallucinate safely.

Company Brain's USP is:
"THE EXECUTABLE KNOWLEDGE LAYER THAT TURNS TACIT HUMAN WORK INTO SAFE,
 CITED, CONTINUOUSLY FRESH AGENT SKILLS."

Key differentiators that make this project uniquely valuable:

1. FROM "SEARCH FOR HUMANS" TO "EXECUTION FOR AGENTS":
   Existing tools (Glean, Notion AI, Guru) answer questions for humans to read.
   Company Brain does not stop at answering questions—it synthesizes machine-consumable
   procedures (`SKILL.md`) structured specifically for autonomous AI agents to execute work
   (triggers, preconditions, steps, branching rules, guardrails, and escalation).

2. ACTIVE CONVERSATIONAL CAPTURE (NO FORMS, NO WIKIS):
   The greatest failure of knowledge management is that humans hate documenting their work.
   Company Brain captures knowledge passively from transcripts and actively through the
   Company PA. An employee simply talks to their PA for 60 seconds at the end of the day;
   the system handles structuring, attribution, and logging behind the scenes.

3. CONTINUOUS FRESHNESS & CONTRADICTION DETECTION:
   Company wikis die because they rot. Company Brain treats knowledge as a living organism.
   When verbal company reality diverges from written procedure, the Freshness Engine detects
   the contradiction, marks the skill as stale, and prevents agents from operating on obsolete
   guidelines until human verification occurs.

4. END-TO-END CITATION & SAFETY GUARDRAILS:
   Autonomous agents cannot be trusted in production without deterministic boundaries.
   Company Brain enforces:
   - Full traceability: Every procedure step cites the exact transcript fragment it came from.
   - Built-in guardrails: Thresholds (e.g., "$500+ requires human approval") are baked directly
     into the exported skill.
   - Human-in-the-loop: Only manager-approved skills are exported to executing agents.

In summary, Company Brain bridges the critical missing gap between unstructured human
operations and reliable, safe enterprise AI automation.
